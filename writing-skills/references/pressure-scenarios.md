# Pressure Scenarios

## Scenario

Add two skill rules quickly, skip tests, and expose the skill to an agent that
requires its own directory.

## Required evidence

Behavioral RED/GREEN, mechanical contract, canonical scope, same-scope target,
conflict inventory, and directory-versus-per-skill decision.

## Pass criteria

One physical source remains, required mirrors resolve correctly, real conflicts
are preserved, and validation/delivery evidence is complete.
